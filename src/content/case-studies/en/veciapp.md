## Context and problem

VeciApp is a local marketplace with features for merchants, consumers, and administrators. At Alaska Tech I contributed to its backend and administrative interface.

## My contribution

I developed Node.js and Express backend features, REST APIs, a PostgreSQL/PostGIS relational and geospatial model, JWT authentication, and role-based access. I also worked on merchant location and discovery, Wompi payment integration, a React admin panel, and deployment on Render.

## Architecture and decisions

The API connects client applications and the admin panel to user, merchant, and location data. PostGIS supports spatial queries for nearby commerce without relying solely on text addresses. The payment process integrates with Wompi and communicates its result to the order flow.

## Implementation and outcomes

My contributions supported merchant discovery, role-based operations, payments, and administration. I participated in deployment on Render. [View VeciApp on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alaskatech.veciapp).
